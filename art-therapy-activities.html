Feelings Thermometer

Kids pick a number and colour to show how strong their emotion feels right now — a simple, visual way to name and track feelings over time.

✋

Hands Past & Future

Children fill hand outlines with images and words — one hand for memories, one for dreams. A gentle way to explore identity and hope.

🎨

Colouring & Mandalas

Detailed colouring pages with patterns, characters, and scenes. Repetitive mark-making calms the nervous system and builds focus and patience.

🔤

Word & Image Play

Fun literacy-linked activities that combine art with language — spotting, sorting, and creating — great for bilingual and early readers.

💬

Emotion Vocabulary

Guided prompts that expand how children describe their inner world — moving beyond "fine" or "mad" into richer emotional language.

📐
