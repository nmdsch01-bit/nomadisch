FAQ

Your questions about the keto diet answered

What is the ketogenic diet (keto diet)? +
The keto diet (ketogenic diet) is a low-carb, high-fat eating plan that shifts your body into a state called ketosis — where you burn fat as your primary fuel instead of carbohydrates. This accelerates weight loss, boosts mental clarity, and dramatically increases energy levels for most people.

Does this work for keto beginners with no experience? +
Absolutely. Keto Creator was designed with beginners in mind. The recipes are simple, the grocery list is straightforward, and the plan walks you through everything step-by-step — from what to eat on a keto diet to how to get into ketosis fast.
Can I do keto if I'm vegetarian or vegan? +
Yes! The quiz asks about your protein preferences, and the algorithm builds a complete vegetarian or vegan keto meal plan — loaded with healthy fat-rich plants, quality protein sources, and delicious keto recipes without meat.
How much weight can I lose on the keto diet? +
Results vary by person. On average, Keto Creator users report losing 8–15 pounds in the first month, with results accelerating as ketosis sets in. The more closely you follow your personalized keto meal plan, the better your results will be.
Is the keto diet safe? What about cholesterol? +
The keto diet is safe for most healthy adults and has been studied extensively. We always recommend consulting your doctor before starting any diet program, especially if you have diabetes, high blood pressure, or other chronic conditions. Our plans are developed with nutritionist-approved macro guidelines.
